# K1 Arrival Reference Lock

Status: **locked by the client on 2026-08-03**

Source: `Docs/ChatGPT Image Aug 3, 2026, 06_24_44 PM (1).png`

This image is the visual north star for Mandegar's opening experience. It defines the world, architecture, framing, material restraint, and pre-activation light state. It is not a flat website background and it is not an exact UI specification.

## Architectural invariants

- One vast circular exhibition hall with a pale, continuous curved envelope.
- One dominant suspended horizontal halo, fully visible and centered over the pavilion.
- A second, smaller suspended cylindrical canopy immediately above the core.
- One central cylindrical pavilion with fine vertical ribbing.
- A low asymmetric curved foreground stage/plinth that partially wraps the core.
- Two attached lateral display pods with rounded rectangular portals.
- A translucent pale curtain or veil behind the core.
- A broad reflective pale floor with a restrained circular inlay around the pavilion.
- Distant, desaturated human silhouettes used only to establish scale.
- Sparse organic foreground silhouettes may frame the shot, but must never become part of the pavilion geometry.

Do not introduce freestanding retail booths, hard-edged trade-show truss, extra floors, stairs, dark industrial structure, dense furniture, or unrelated architecture.

## K1 camera and composition

- Frontal, centered, near-symmetrical establishing view.
- Slightly elevated human eye level; the pavilion remains approachable rather than monumental.
- Provisional lens language: wide architectural perspective without fisheye distortion.
- Keep the complete main halo inside frame and make it the largest graphic shape.
- Keep the pavilion centered beneath the halo with generous negative space above and around it.
- The curved stage crosses the lower third of the pavilion and does not obscure the ribbed core.
- Foreground silhouettes may occupy the extreme lower corners only.

Derived modeling views may move the camera, but they must preserve the same module count, attachment points, proportions, hierarchy, and materials.

## Materials and light

- Warm white, pale gray, soft silver, and low-saturation translucent materials.
- Matte architectural shells with softly rounded edges.
- Fine vertical ribbing on the central volume; no coarse slats.
- Softly reflective floor, not a mirror and not wet.
- Luminous elements feel built into the architecture rather than applied as neon strips.
- K1 is a calm pre-activation state: warm-white only, low contrast, bright atmosphere, soft silver shadows.
- The surrounding hall stays quieter than the pavilion so the halo and core remain dominant.

## Continuity rules for K2-K7

- The architecture and camera path stay continuous; only light, media, people, and controlled motion evolve.
- K2 Discovery introduces subtle responsive traces without changing the structure.
- K3 Activation wakes elements in a fixed order: central screen, media wall, booths/branding, spatial light paths.
- K4 Full Reveal carries the event color and strongest media state while preserving the pale material world.
- Later invitation and loop states reduce intensity without cutting to a different location.
- No dark sci-fi, gaming, cyberpunk, or neon-dominant treatment at any phase.
- Desktop and portrait crops must depict the same pavilion and activation moment, not alternative designs.

## UI exclusions

The generated header, navigation, language control, corner prompts, logo drawing, Persian labels, and scroll indicator in the reference are composition placeholders. They must not be copied literally. Production UI must use the project's real localized content, accessible controls, and approved Mandegar identity.

## Approval criteria for derived images

A derived view is approved only when it preserves:

1. the dominant halo-to-pavilion scale relationship;
2. the central ribbed cylinder, smaller canopy, curved stage, veil, and exactly two attached side pods;
3. the pale restrained material and lighting language;
4. spatial continuity with K1; and
5. a buildable architectural reading suitable for modeling.

The first generated three-quarter attempt is retained as a mood study because it did not rotate far enough to prove the pavilion geometry. It must not be used as a modeling elevation.
