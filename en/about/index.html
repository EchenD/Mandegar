03 / From thought to experience

A clear path through the work.

Each project has its own needs. These stages create a shared starting point for decisions, development and delivery.

  1. 01

    Understand the brief

    Clarify the audience, ambition, setting and constraints before choosing a direction.

  2. 02

    Shape the direction

    Explore an idea and define the scope, responsibilities and review points together.

  3. 03

    Develop and test

    Build the agreed elements and check how they work in the intended setting.

  4. 04

    Prepare for delivery

    Align final materials, operation guidance and handover with the agreed plan.

04 / Working together

A collaboration shaped around the brief.

Location, language, venue conditions and local requirements all influence delivery. The working model and any partner involvement are discussed for each project before responsibilities are agreed.

05 / People & collaboration

The right people around the idea.

Creative direction, production and technical development can come together around the needs of each project.

Creative direction

From the audience and central story to a visual language, a visitor journey and the details that give an idea its shape.

Space & production

From scale and materials to a build plan, delivery coordination and preparing the experience for its audience.

Content & technology

From visual storytelling to digital tools, interaction and testing technical elements in the intended setting.
