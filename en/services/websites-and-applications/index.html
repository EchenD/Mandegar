A few useful answers

What should an initial brief include?+

A short description of the idea, audience, location, timing and known requirements is a useful start. An early brief can be developed together.

Can one discipline be part of a wider project?+

A brief may focus on one discipline or connect several. The working arrangement and responsibility for each part are discussed before scope is agreed.

How are timing and deliverables confirmed?+

They depend on the brief, venue, content needs and technical requirements. The proposal records the agreed scope and schedule after these have been reviewed.
